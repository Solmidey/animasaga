It was said that during this celestial event, the fabric of reality would be at its most fragile, and the balance of power would shift in ways both unseen and unprecedented.

Lena, a skilled warrior from the Flame faction, stood atop a ruined spire, gazing out at the city she had sworn to protect. The air around him seemed to vibrate with an otherworldly energy, as if the very fabric of reality was being warped by his presence.

She was a warrior, a protector, and a witness to the unfolding drama of Elyndra.
